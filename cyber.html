Common Cybercrime Problems

🎣 Phishing Attacks

Fake emails and messages are used to trick people into sharing personal information.

💳 Online Payment Fraud

Fraudsters may trick people into making unauthorized online payments.

🔐 Identity Theft

Someone misuses another person's personal information without permission.

📱 Social Media Scams

Fake profiles, suspicious links and misleading offers can deceive users.

Cybercrime Prevention Solutions

Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords for different accounts and protect them.

Two-Factor Authentication

Enable two-factor authentication to improve account security.

Avoid Suspicious Links

Never open unknown links or download suspicious attachments.

Protect Personal Information

Never share passwords, OTPs or confidential banking details.

What to Do If You Face Cybercrime?

  1. Stay calm and stop interacting with the suspicious account.
  2. Save relevant messages, screenshots and transaction records.
  3. Contact your bank immediately if money is involved.
  4. Change compromised passwords and secure your accounts.
  5. Report financial cyber fraud by calling 1930 promptly.
  6. Submit an official complaint at the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
